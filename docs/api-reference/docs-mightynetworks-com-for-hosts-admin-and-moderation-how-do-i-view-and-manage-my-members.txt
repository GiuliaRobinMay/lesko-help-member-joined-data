Accessing Your Member Lists
​
Network Member List
To see a full list of members in your Mighty Network go to Admin > Members
​
Space Member List
To see a full list of members in a Space, click into the Space and go to Manage > Members
If you’re on the Launch Plan or higher, you’ll also see a “See Insights” button. Clicking this takes you to the Members tab in Mighty Insights , where you can view analytics like Contributions , Contributing Members , and more.
You can also use the search bar (upper right corner) to find members by name or email.
​

The date the account was created. This could be different from the join date if you have approvals turned on or if the account creation was abandoned at any point.
Joined Network Date
Last Active
​
Filters
You can filter your list by:
Spaces: Shows which members belong to a specific Space.
Plans: Shows which members are part of specific Plans.
Member Type: Host, Moderator, Full Member, or Limited Member.
Tags and Badges: Available on the Launch Plan ** **and higher.
Audience: Filter by an audience you’ve defined for promotional banners.
Inactive Members: Shows members who haven’t logged in within the last 30 days.
You can re-engage them using Activity Assist (learn more in this article).
Add Filter: Sort by specific Custom Fields you’ve created.
​
Sorting Options
You can sort your member list by:
Name: A–Z or Z–A
Role
Date Joined: Earliest or Latest
Last Active: Earliest or Latest
​
Menu Options
In the three dot menu next to the member’s name, you have options to:
Reach out: opens a DM with the person
Invite to Space
More
Block Member
Report Member
View Member Detail
Add Tag
Add Badge
Change Network Role
View as Member
More
Remove from Everything
Ban from Network
​
Bulk Actions
From your Network or Space member list, you can perform bulk actions, such as:
Sending a message to multiple members
Adding or removing Tags and Badges
Adding to Networks and Space(s )
Note: To perform bulk actions, you must be a Network Host/Moderator or a Space Host/Moderator for that specific Space.
