Can I Download My Member Data?
Copy page Copy page
Copy page Copy page
When you upgrade to The Launch Plan or higher, you unlock full access to data about your members—like their email addresses, profiles, Space memberships, Ambassador status, and more.
You can download this data anytime as an Excel (.xlsx) file for:
Your entire Mighty Network
An individual Space you host
Each paid Plan , if you’ve enabled payments
This data helps you better understand your members and grow your community.
​
What You Can Do with Member Data
Import it into an external CRM system
Add members to your email newsletter list
Measure engagement or track course completion rates
Understand who your members are and how they interact
​
How to Download Your Member List
Go to Admin > Members > Member List .
Click Download .
Confirm you’ll follow Mighty Networks’ Terms of Use (no selling or sharing member data).
You’ll get an email with a download link for your up-to-date member list.
To learn how to view and manage your Member List, check out this article .

What’s in the Space Member List
You can download a Space-specific list if you’re both a Network Host and a Space Host .
Important note: You will need to be a Network Host and a Space Host to be able to download the member list. For example, if you are a Space Host and a Network member, then you will not be able to download the member list for the Space.
To download: Go to the Space → Settings > Member List > Download Member List .
Columns include:
Member ID
First Name
Last Name
Email Address

Joined Space (this is recorded based on UTC Time Zone)

Last Visited Course (this is recorded based on UTC Time Zone)
Course Completion %

Last Viewed Lesson

What’s in the Plan Member List
If you are a Network Host of a Network with Plans , you can also download a Plan-specific list to see who joined or purchased a plan.
To download:

You’ll get two tabs:
