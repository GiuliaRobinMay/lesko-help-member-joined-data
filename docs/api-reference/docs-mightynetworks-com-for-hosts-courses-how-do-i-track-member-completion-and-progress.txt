How Do I Track Member Completion and Progress?
Copy page Copy page
Copy page Copy page
When creating or managing Lessons inside the Course feature , you can set both unlocking and completion requirements. These settings help guide members through your Course in the order and pace you choose.
To update settings for a Lesson, open the Lesson and select Manage Lesson Settings .
​
Unlock Options for Lessons
These are the unlock options available today:
​
None
The Lesson is available immediately when the member starts the Course.
​
Sequential
Members unlock the Lesson only after completing the previous one.This is a per-user experience, so unlock timing varies by member.
​
Timed
The Lesson unlocks a set number of days after a member joins the Space.
Unlocks at the exact join time (e.g., joined at 12pm → unlocks at 12pm).
Members are notified when new material unlocks.
If new Lessons are added after a member joins, timers still apply to the original join date.
If a member leaves and rejoins, the timer still uses the first join date.
Bulk-selecting Timed Unlock applies the same unlock day to all selected Lessons.
To stagger unlocks (e.g., Day 7 → Day 14 → Day 21), set each Lesson individually.
​
Scheduled
The Lesson unlocks on a specific date and time.
To keep it hidden until the unlock date, set the Lesson to Hidden .
If Visible, members can see the Lesson title but can’t access content until the unlock time.
​
Important
Any unlock changes apply to existing members and new members .
To change the Unlock Settings for multiple Lessons at once, hover your cursor over a Lesson and tick the box to the left. You’ll then see the option to select multiple Lessons or Select All.
​
What Locked Lessons Look Like
Locked Lessons will have a time icon, when hovering over it will show when the Lesson will unlock.
​
Unlock Notifications
For Scheduled and Timed unlocks, you can choose whether members receive a notification.
Toggle ON → Members receive a notification when the Lesson unlocks.
Toggle OFF → No notification is sent.
This toggle is disabled for all other unlock types.
​
Completion Tracking for Lessons
These are the available completion options:
​
None
The completion of the Lesson won’t be tracked.
​
Visited
A member must open the Lesson to complete it.
​
Button
A member must click Mark as Complete .
Note: If Unlock Settings = None , Button Completion does not block access to the next Lesson. To require completion, use Sequential unlocking.
​
Video
A member must watch the entire tracked video.
You can track native MP4 uploads or embedded YouTube videos .
Loom, Vimeo, and Wistia cannot be tracked.
Only one tracked video per Lesson.
Choose the tracked video using the timer icon .
Members may fast-forward through both embedded and native videos.
Video resumes where members left off only when tracking is enabled.
​
Additional Notes
You cannot reset completion on behalf of a member.
Edits to completion settings apply immediately to all members.
If you add a new video and enable tracking, members who previously completed the Lesson stay completed .
If you choose any of the completion options, there will be a checkmark next to the Lesson once it’s been completed.

Measuring Member Completion
If completion tracking is on, you can see each member’s progress at a glance.
Go to:
Space Settings > Members > Member List
You’ll see:
% complete
Last Lesson or Quiz viewed
Last Lesson or Quiz completed
Completion data also appears in the downloadable member list and inside the Members feature (if enabled in the Space).
​
Reset Progress
You can also allow members to reset their Course progress:
Go to the Space’s gear icon .
Select Features > Course > Reset Progress and toggle it on.
When enabled, members can reset their progress:
From the Next Up card (three-dot menu).
On both web and mobile.
​
What happens when progress is reset:
Lesson progress and Quiz attempts are cleared.
Locked content may become locked again.
Hosts cannot reset progress on a member’s behalf.
Previous Quiz attempts still appear in the CSV export.
If a removed member rejoins with the same account, their previous Course progress is restored.
​
Understanding the “Next Up” Section
The Next Up section guides members through the Course based on your settings.It appears only when:
Completion tracking is enabled, and
Unlock or completion rules apply.
Space Hosts do not see Next Up (members only).
