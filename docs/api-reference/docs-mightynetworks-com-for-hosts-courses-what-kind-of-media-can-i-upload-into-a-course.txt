What Kind of Media Can I Upload Into a Course?
Copy page Copy page
Copy page Copy page
You can add several types of content to your Course, including images, videos, media, and file attachments. These can appear in your Overview , Sections, and Lessons .
​
Adding a Primary Image or Video
First, you will add a hero image or video URL at the top of your content.
Recommended image size: 2560 x 1440 pixels (16:9 ratio)
Supported video platforms: YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, and more
To delete or change media, select the media and use the trash icon.
​
Formatting Text
Highlight any text in the editor to:
Add headers (five levels available)
Apply bold , italic , strikethrough, or underline
Add quotes or change text color and highlight color
Insert hyperlinks
​
Adding Rich Media
To upload media within your Course:
Press Enter to create a new line.
Select the + button on the left side of the editor.
Choose to add a photo , video , media embed , file attachment , or section divider .
To add a line of text below a media item, hover under it and click the arrow that appears.
​
How to Upload a Video
Open the Overview , a Section , or a Lesson .
Click the + icon to open the quick insert toolbar.
Select Video.
Choose your video file. You’ll see a progress bar during upload, and the file will appear greyed out while it processes.
Once processing is complete, you can update the preview thumbnail, enable video tracking, and save.
​
Video Tracking
Video Tracking allows you to require members to watch an entire video before they can move to the next Lesson.​ Learn more about completion features in Courses.
​
Upload Limitations
Files larger than 250 MB cannot be uploaded.
As a Host, you can decide if you want your members to upload 250MB or keep them at a smaller file upload limit: 25MB. You can make this decision in Admin > Member Settings
Videos larger than 4 GB cannot be uploaded.
​
Additional Notes
• The max video upload size of 4 GB applies to: Network Hosts
• The max file upload size of 25 MB applies to: Network Hosts, Space Hosts or Space moderators.
Storage limits: On newer plans (Explore, Launch, Scale, Growth), course videos count toward your Network’s storage limit. Legacy plans with unlimited course videos are not affected by this change.
Videos cannot be downloaded from a Course.
Supported file types include .mp4 , .mov , .m4v , and many others (full list below).
You can’t save or close content while a video is uploading. You can save during processing and navigate elsewhere in your Mighty Network. You’ll receive a notification when processing finishes.
You can continue embedding video from other platforms. Previously embedded videos don’t need to be changed unless you prefer to upload them natively.
Members must watch the entire tracked video to unlock the next Lesson or resource.
Playback speed controls are available for both natively uploaded videos and YouTube embeds on web and mobile web.
If a video is paused, it resumes where the member left off— Video Tracking must be turned on for this to work.
​
Supported File Types
video/mp4: mp4, mpg4, f4v, f4p, mp4v
video/webm: webm
video/avi: avi
video/ogg: ogg, ogv
application/ogg: ogx
video/mpeg: mp2, mp3g, mpe, mpeg, mpg, m1v, m2v
video/3gpp: 3gp, 3gpp
video/x-ms-asf: asf, asx
video/x-ms-wmv: wmv
video/quicktime: qt, mov
video/mp2t: ts, mts, m2ts, cpi, clpi, mpl, mpls, bdm
video/x-flv: flv
video/x-matroska: mk3d, mks, mkv
application/mxf: mxf

You can add hashtags (#) to connect related Course content.Start typing a hashtag, and a list of existing hashtags or highlighted ones in your Space will appear.
