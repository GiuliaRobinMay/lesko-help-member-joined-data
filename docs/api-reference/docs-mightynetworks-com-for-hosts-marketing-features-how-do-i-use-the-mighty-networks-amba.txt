How Do I Use the Mighty Networks Ambassador Program?
Copy page Copy page
Copy page Copy page
Your Ambassador Program is a built-in member referral system that encourages and rewards members for inviting others to join your Mighty Network.
The Ambassador Program becomes available when your Mighty Network is on the Launch Plan or higher. It’s designed to motivate members to share your Network based on its value and impact—not through paid commissions.
If you’re looking for a paid affiliate model, you can connect an affiliate integration alongside the Ambassador Program.
Learn more about affiliates in this article .
​
How to Enable the Ambassador Program
By default, the Ambassador Program is turned off.
To enable it:
Go to Admin > Members > Ambassador Program.
Toggle the feature on.
​
When you enable your Ambassador Program:
You can send an announcement to your current members by going to Manage > Send an Announcement.
New members automatically receive an in-app notification and email encouraging them to refer others.
Each member gets a personalized referral link , available anytime from the Invite page or their profile in the mobile app.
Note: If a member shares their referral link directly, it won’t appear in the Invites Sent list. That list only tracks invitations sent through the built-in email feature.
You can track your Ambassadors and their referrals from your Members page.
Hosts on the Launch Plan and up can download the Member List to see detailed referral data.
Members can view their referral count and Ambassador level on the Invite page.
To send an announcement, your Mighty Network must have at least one member. If you’ve already sent one, you’ll need to disable and re-enable the Ambassador Program to send another.
​
How the Ambassador Program Works
When members refer new people who join your Mighty Network, they automatically earn an Ambassador level:
Bronze Ambassador: 2+ referrals
Silver Ambassador: 10+ referrals
Gold Ambassador: 25+ referrals
As members level up, they receive a notification and can see their status on their profile.
Once enabled, Ambassador levels can’t be renamed or reset.
​
If Your Mighty Network Is Accessible Through Plans
Members can share invites from the Invite page , Invite icon (on web), or their referral link .
Invitees skip the landing page and go straight to sign-up, where they select a Plan.
Once the invitee joins a Plan (or completes a free trial, if offered), the referring member earns credit.
Invitees aren’t automatically granted free access to paid Plans. To let them join for free, you can send them an invite link for a hidden Free Plan .
​
If Your Mighty Network Is Invite-Only
Members can invite others from the Invite page , Invite icon (on web), or their referral link .
Invitees can join your Mighty Network without approval.
When they sign up, the referring member automatically receives credit.
​
Adding Custom Rewards
You can add your own rewards to recognize Ambassadors as they reach new levels.
To do this:
Go to Admin > Members > Ambassador Program > Manage Additional Ambassador Rewards.
Add rewards and instructions for each level.
Examples of rewards Hosts often use:
10% off a community shop
A free tote bag or t-shirt
A featured “Member of the Month” spotlight
A complimentary event ticket
Example messages to share when a member levels up:
“You’re now a Silver Ambassador! Enjoy 10% off in our online store using this code: XYZ .”
“Congrats on referring 10 new members! Here’s your early access code for our next event: XYZ .”
“You rock! A Host will reach out soon to feature you as our Member of the Month.”
You can also automate these rewards using our Automations or with Zapier when members reach a new level.
You can update or disable rewards anytime.
​
Turning Off the Ambassador Program
To turn off the Ambassador Program:
Go to Admin > Members > Ambassador Program.
Toggle the feature off.
​
Tracking Ambassador Data
To view referral data and Ambassador activity, visit your Ambassadors Dashboard in Mighty Insights™ .
