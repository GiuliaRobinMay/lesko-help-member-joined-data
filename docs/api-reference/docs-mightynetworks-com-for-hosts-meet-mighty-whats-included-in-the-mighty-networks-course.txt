What’s Included in the Mighty Networks Launch Plan?

Learn all about the Launch plan
Copy page Copy page
Welcome! We’re so glad you’re exploring Mighty and learning more about the Launch Plan .
The Launch Plan is designed to help you get started with focus . It’s ideal if you’re launching your first offer, validating an idea, or building early momentum in your Mighty Network—without unnecessary complexity.
By intentionally limiting options at the beginning, the Launch Plan helps you drive engagement faster and build a strong foundation you can scale later.
​
What You Get with the Launch Plan
The Launch Plan gives you everything you need to launch and run your Mighty Network, including:
Your own Mighty Network website , 100% ad-free
Free Mighty apps on iOS and Android for you and your members
The ability to charge for membership in hundreds of local currencies
Direct messaging between members
Chat inside each Space
Unlimited Spaces
Posts, Articles, and hashtags for ongoing discussion and content
On top of that, the Launch Plan includes:
Feed, Chat, Events, or Course features
3 Hosts
10 Moderators
Native video uploads for Posts, Articles, and course lessons
Polls & Questions for quick, lightweight engagement
Events you can host online or in person
Course creation, including video lessons
AI features powered by People Magic
200 GB of storage, including course video
Livestreaming — up to 20 hours/month with 100 attendees per stream
Your own custom domain
Mighty Insights™ analytics
Member list export and segmentation with Member Tags
Built-in member referral program
Profile Badges, Recognitions & Points, and Basic Leaderboards
Audience-filtered and Courses & Gamification automations
Google Analytics and marketing pixel integration
​
Launch Plan Pricing
The Launch Plan is available at:
$79 per month when paid annually
$95 per month when paid monthly
​
Starting a Free Trial
If you’re new to Mighty, you’ll start with a free 14-day trial of the Growth Plan .
This lets you explore higher limits and advanced features before choosing the plan that’s right for your Mighty Network—including the Launch Plan.
​
Add More with Engagement Boosts
Need additional storage, livestreaming hours, or participant capacity?
You can add a monthly Engagement Boost directly from your Mighty Network:
Profile Picture > Billing Plans > Storage and Usage
​
Want Your Own Branded Mobile Apps?
With Mighty Pro , you can launch your Mighty Network as your own branded app—available in the Apple App Store and Google Play Store under your name and logo.
