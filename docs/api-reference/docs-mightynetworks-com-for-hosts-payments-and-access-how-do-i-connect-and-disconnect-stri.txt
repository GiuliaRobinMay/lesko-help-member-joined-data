How Do I Connect and Disconnect Stripe to My Mighty Network?
Copy page Copy page
Copy page Copy page
Mighty Networks uses Stripe as our third-party payment processor to ensure all transactions are secure and reliable.
When you connect your own Stripe account, you’ll get paid directly through Stripe—meaning Mighty Networks never stores or processes your members’ credit card details. Stripe currently supports credit cards, debit cards, and prepaid cards .
You can manage your payments, refunds, and customer details directly in Stripe.​
Learn more about Stripe on stripe.com .
Note: Stripe is available in 46 countries. You can view supported countries here .
At this time, Mighty Networks does not support PayPal or other payment processors.
​
How to Connect Your Stripe Account
Go to Admin > Payments > Stripe — the direct path is /admin/stripe .
Choose Connect Stripe Account .
Follow the prompts to either:
Connect an existing Stripe account, or
Create a new Stripe account directly from the setup screen.
Once connected, your Mighty Network is ready to process payments, subscriptions, and one-time purchases instantly.
​
Changing or Disconnecting Your Stripe Account
If you only need to change the bank account where Stripe sends payouts, you can do this directly in your Stripe dashboard without disconnecting from Mighty Networks.
Refer to Stripe’s Help Center guide for step-by-step instructions, or contact Stripe Support if you need additional help.
If you need to disconnect your Stripe account completely:
Click the Need Help button in the bottom right corner of your Mighty Network.
Send a message to our team who will assist you with next steps.
​
Important Notes About Stripe
​
Manage Plans Only in Mighty Networks
Always manage, edit, or deactivate your Plans inside Mighty Networks—never in Stripe.
Why this matters: Making changes to Plans directly in Stripe can cause serious issues, including loss of subscribed members and deletion of Plans.
You can still:
View transactions
Manage individual payments
Issue refunds
…all directly within your Stripe account.
​
Re-Enabling Payments
If you deactivate payments and later decide to re-enable them, you’ll need to connect a new Stripe account when setting up payments again. You can’t reuse the same account that was connected previously.
​
Multiple Mighty Networks
Each Mighty Network must have its own Stripe account connection .
If you manage multiple Networks:
Create a separate Stripe account for each one.
You can manage multiple Stripe accounts under a single email address by using Stripe’s “multiple accounts” feature .
Each Stripe account can only be linked to one Mighty Network at a time. This ensures that notifications like failed payments are properly routed to the correct Network.
