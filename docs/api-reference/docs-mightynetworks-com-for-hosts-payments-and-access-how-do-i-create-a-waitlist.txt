How Do I Create a Waitlist?
Copy page Copy page
Copy page Copy page
Waitlists let you collect interest before your Mighty Network, a new Plan, or an upcoming offering is ready to launch.
Instead of sending members to an experience that isn’t available yet, you can create a waitlist, add it to a landing page, and start building your audience before launch.
Note: Waitlists are currently available on web only.
​
Create a Waitlist
You can create a waitlist from a Landing Page or directly from the Waitlists page in Admin.
​
From a Landing Page
Go to Admin > Marketing > Landing Pages .
Create a new landing page or edit an existing one.
Add a Waitlist section.
Choose Create New Waitlist (or you can choose an existing one)
Complete your waitlist settings:
Internal Name (for your reference)
Customize the confirmation message -this is the message that people who join the waitlist will receive
Close Date and Time (optional)
If you would like to have a countdown timer, choose this option
Auto-Invite on Close (optional)
If you enable Auto-Invite on Close , choose whether people should be invited to:
Your Mighty Network, or
A specific Plan and where you would like them to land :
Plan Landing Page
Create Account Page
Sign in Page
Customize the invitation message that will be sent when the waitlist closes.
Save your waitlist.
​
From the Waitlists Page
Go to Admin > Marketing > Waitlists .
Select Create Waitlist .
Then follow the same steps as above to setup the waitlist.
​
Customize Your Waitlist
Once your waitlist has been created, you can customize how it appears on your Landing Page.
You can edit:
Heading and description
Call-to-action button text
Background image
Optional countdown timer
The colors that it shows are from your Network branding colors .
When you’re ready, click Save.
​
Waitlist Notifications
​
Host notifications
As a Host, you will receive a notification (email, in-app or push) whenever someone joins a waitlist.
This notification is turned on by default and you can manage it by going to Personal Settings > Notifications
​
Waitlist confirmation emails
When someone joins your waitlist, they’ll automatically receive a confirmation email letting them know they’ve successfully signed up. The email also includes an unsubscribe link.
If they are a full member, they will also get an in-app or push notification.
Tip: We recommend joining your own waitlist with a test email address to confirm both the signup confirmation and confirmation email look the way you expect.
​
Managing Waitlists
To manage your waitlists, go to:
Admin > Marketing > Waitlists
From here you can:
View all waitlists
Edit waitlists
Sort waitlists
View waitlist leads
Manually close a waitlist
Delete a waitlist
​
Manage Leads
All waitlist signups are available in:
Admin > Members > Leads
This page also includes Sent Invites and Requests to Join , making it the central place to manage prospective members.
From the Leads page you can:
Search by email address
Filter by:
Date
Status
Waitlist
Source landing page
On the Explore plan and above you will be able to
See lead emails
Invite individual leads
Send bulk invitations
Download leads as a CSV
​
Sync Waitlists with Kit
If you use the Kit integration , you can automatically sync waitlist signups.
To enable syncing:
Go to Admin > Integrations > Kit .
Turn on Sync Waitlist Emails .
Note: syncing will not be on automatically and emails will not sync retroactively
Waitlist signups are sent to Kit with the following tags:
Waitlist Name
Mighty Networks Integration
Once someone creates an account, their Network tag will also be added.
After enabling the integration, create a test signup to verify that new waitlist leads are syncing correctly.
​
Close a Waitlist
You can manually close a waitlist at any time.
Go to Admin > Marketing > Waitlists .
Select your waitlist.
Click Close Waitlist .
If Auto-Invite on Close is enabled, everyone on the waitlist will automatically receive your invitation email.
​
Frequently Asked Questions
​
Do people need a Mighty Networks account to join a waitlist?
No. Anyone can join a waitlist by submitting their email address on your landing page.
​
Can I invite people later?
Yes. You can invite individual leads or send invitations in bulk from Admin > Members > Leads .
​
Can I automatically invite everyone when my waitlist closes?
Yes. Enable Auto-Invite on Close when creating your waitlist.
​
Can I add Leads to a Space, plan etc?
At this time, Leads haven’t created a full account yet, so they can’t be managed as full or limited members.
​
Can people see where they are in line?
No. Waitlists do not display a person’s position.
​
Can I limit the number of people on a waitlist?
Waitlists do not currently support capacity limits.
​
Can I create a waitlist for an individual Space?
Waitlists are added to landing pages and can invite people to your Network or a specific Plan. They are not available for individual Spaces.
