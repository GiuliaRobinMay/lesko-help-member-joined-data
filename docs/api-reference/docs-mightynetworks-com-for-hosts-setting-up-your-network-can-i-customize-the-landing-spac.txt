Can I Customize the Landing Space for My Members?
Copy page Copy page
Copy page Copy page
Landing Spaces let you curate where people land when they enter your Mighty Network. You can set different Landing Spaces for new members, returning members, and visitors.
To get started, go to Admin > Spaces & Navigation > Landing Spaces . From there, you can set one, two, or all three Landing Space options.
Only Spaces set to Anyone Can Join and auto join will appear in the dropdown menu.
​
Landing Space for New Members
The Space you select is where someone lands the first time they enter your Mighty Network after creating an account.
If a member is invited through a specific Space, they will land in the Space they were invited to instead of the Landing Space for New Members.
The selected Space must be Anyone Can Join and auto join .
​
Landing Space for Returning Members
The Space you choose becomes the default Space for:
Returning members
Network Hosts, Space Hosts, Network moderators, and Space moderators
Members will see this Landing Space when they sign in on a new device or return after signing out. If a member stays signed in, their last-visited Space will load instead.
​
Landing Space for Visitors
This option appears only in Anyone Can Join Mighty Networks.
The selected Space is where visitors—people who have not created an account—will land when they first view your Mighty Network. The Space must be Anyone Can Join and auto join .
If no Landing Space is set, visitors and returning members will land on the first Global Feature, first joined Space, or first Collection in the Left Navigation Panel.
​
Plan Landing Space
If you want to customize the Landing Space for members who join through a plan, you can set it up during plan creation .
You can also edit this anytime by going to Admin > Plans > Plan List , selecting the plan, and opening the Settings tab. Under Spaces & Audiences , you’ll find the Plan Landing Space setting.
