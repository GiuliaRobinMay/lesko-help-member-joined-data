How Do I Chat with Cohost?

Copy page Copy page
Mighty Cohost is your all-in-one assistant for building and running your community.
Available on the Explore Plan and above, Cohost is trained on our Community Design ™ framework, help center and more!
It’s designed to help you design, launch, and grow a thriving Mighty Network—while answering your questions 24/7.
​
Chat with Cohost
In the upper right corner of your Mighty Network on web, you’ll find the Cohost icon next to your personal settings.
On mobile, you’ll see it on your bottom navigation
All Network Hosts can view and participate in Cohost conversations, however you will only see your messages with Cohost. You can rate a response with a thumbs-up or thumbs-down to help improve future results.
The two icons next to the Cohost name will show you:
Clock icon — Your conversation history and updates from the Mighty team
Close icon — Closes the Cohost panel
​
What you can do with Cohost
Cohost helps you take action and move faster. Here’s what it can do today. For actions that need confirmation, you’ll see an approval card first — more on that below .
​
Create and set up
Spaces — Create new Spaces using available templates
Collections — Create Collections and add new Spaces to them
Rename — rename existing Spaces and Collections, your Network name
Reorder — Spaces in existing Collections
Web address — change your Network’s mn.co subdomain after confirming the exact new address
Plans — Set up pricing and monetization for your Network, including:
Naming
Pricing
Gates
Sales pitch
Benefits list
Descriptions
​
Customize your Network
Update your Big Purpose
Store and refine your community design (Ideal Member, goals, themes, and more) to personalize guidance
​
Get guidance and support
Search the Help Center for step-by-step articles
Get direct links to the right settings pages
Help with strategies for community building, growth, and engagement
​
Take action, with your approval
Send announcements and manage invite requests
Manage members, Badges, Tags, and Profile Fields
Manage Course Spaces, Events, and Landing Pages
Manage plans and subscriptions
Manage Marketing Banners
​
Find your way faster
For actions like renaming, reordering, or deleting, Cohost will guide you to the exact place in your settings so you can complete it in just a few clicks.
​
Approve important actions before they happen
Cohost can now take on more of the work, and you always keep the final say. When you ask Cohost to do something that needs confirmation, it pauses and shows you an approval card in chat first. Nothing happens until you tap Approve.
If you’d rather not move forward, just leave the card alone. Cohost won’t take the action without your approval.
​
Actions Cohost can take with your approval
Ask Cohost to handle any of the following, and it’ll show you an approval card first:
Announcements and invites
Send an announcement to members
Approve or reject a join request, individually or in bulk
Members
Remove a member from your Network (they can rejoin later)
Delete a Badge
Delete a Tag
Delete a Profile Field
Delete an option from a Profile Field
Spaces and events
Delete a Course Space and its contents
Cancel a single occurrence of a recurring Event
Unpublish a Landing Page
Payments and plans
Cancel a member’s subscription (takes effect at the end of their current billing period)
Remove a member’s access to a plan
Delete a plan group (the plans inside it stay intact)
Archive a plan (archived plans can’t be reactivated)
Marketing
Delete a Marketing Banner
Cohost conversations work independently of your Network’s private chat settings . Even if private chat is turned off for members, you can still chat with Cohost.
Message limits by plan:
Explore Plan: 25 messages/day
Launch Plan: 100 messages/day
Scale Plan and above: Unlimited messages
