Frequently asked questions
Quick Answers to your questions about Mighty Partners
Who can become a Mighty Partner?
You can join from anywhere in the world, as long as you are eligible for a PayPal account, as this is how commissions are paid. The program is open to anyone with a relevant audience (marketers, consultants, content creators, agencies, educators, etc.). You do not need to be a Mighty host to join.
How long is the referral window for earning commission on sales?
Our cookie window is 60 days, meaning if someone clicks your promotion link and purchases Mighty (from anywhere) within 60 days, you'll be awarded commission for their purchase.
How do I track my referrals and commissions on referred sales?
Once you're accepted into the Mighty Partner Program, you'll get set up in our Partner portal, where you can easily view referrals and commissions.
What if I have questions or need help?
Once you're a partner, you'll get a dedicated Partner Manager who solely focuses on your experience as a Mighty Partner. You can reach out to them at any time for questions and requests!
How is this different from the Mighty Expert Certification?
The Partner Program is for creators, entrepreneurs, consultants, and agencies that want to earn affiliate commissions by sharing Mighty Networks with their audience and collaborate with a community of people. The Mighty Certified Expert Program is a training and certification program for those who want to offer services helping clients build, market, and manage their communities on Mighty. This comprehensive training program focuses on helping these consultants master the Mighty Networks platform and best practices for building and growing communities. If you are interested in becoming a Mighty Certified Expert, you can find out more about the program here .
What are the terms and conditions of the program?
Our Partner Program T&Cs can be found here .
Are there any training materials available?
Yes. You'll get access to training videos and other resources to help you learn the Mighty platform, the power of community, and Mighty community-building frameworks!
Can I promote using social media, email, or ads?
You can promote Mighty via social media platforms, blogs, newsletters, YouTube, paid ads—anywhere you wish as long as you follow our terms of use.
Mighty offers a 14-day free trial. How can I earn commission once they pay?
Each Mighty Host begins their experience with a 14-day free trial. After the 14 days are complete, they can then select a plan and pay annually or monthly. Once a plan is selected (and they have used your link within 60 days), you'll get commission on this purchase - recurring every time they are charged!
